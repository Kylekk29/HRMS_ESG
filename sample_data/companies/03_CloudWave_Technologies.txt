=== Company Profile: CloudWave Technologies ===

Industry: Cloud Infrastructure / DevOps Solutions
Founded: 2020
Headquarters: Taipei, Taiwan
Employees: 48
CEO: Jennifer Chen

=== Mission ===
Simplify cloud-native infrastructure so engineers can focus on product.

=== Core Values ===
1. Reliability — Our platform must be boringly stable.
2. Developer Empathy — We build tools we'd want to use ourselves.
3. Transparency — Open source, open roadmap, open communication.
4. Efficiency — Optimize for impact, not hours worked.
5. Diversity — Different perspectives build better products.

=== Culture & Work Style ===
- Office-first (Taipei), remote-friendly for senior ICs
- Stand-up desks, nap pods, and a well-stocked kitchen
- No fixed working hours — trust-based autonomy
- Weekly tech talks and knowledge-sharing sessions
- English and Mandarin bilingual work environment
- Monthly team social events (board games, karaoke, hiking)

=== Engineering Culture ===
- Trunk-based development with feature flags
- Everything runs on Kubernetes (their own product)
- Chaos engineering Fridays
- Blameless culture with thorough incident reviews
- Pair programming for complex features
- All engineers have on-call rotation (1 week per month)

=== HR Policies ===
- Probation: 3 months
- Annual leave: 15 days (starting), up to 22 days after 3 years
- Sick leave: 30 days half-pay
- Full health and dental insurance
- Professional development budget: NT$60,000/year
- Free lunch and snacks in office
- Employee stock options available
- Conference attendance fully covered (2 per year)
