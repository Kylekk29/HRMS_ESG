Data Scientist

Job Description:
We are looking for a talented Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will work on developing machine learning models, analyzing large datasets, and driving data-informed product decisions.

Required Qualifications:
• 3+ years of experience in data science or related field
• Proficiency in Python and data science libraries (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn)
• Experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
• Strong SQL skills and experience with large-scale data processing
• Experience with data visualization tools (Matplotlib, Plotly, Tableau)
• Solid understanding of statistics and experimental design
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field

Preferred Qualifications:
• Master's or PhD in a quantitative field
• Experience with NLP or computer vision
• Experience with MLOps and model deployment
• Knowledge of big data technologies (Spark, Dask)
• Experience with A/B testing and causal inference
• Published research or contributions to open-source ML projects

Responsibilities:
• Develop and deploy machine learning models to production
• Analyze large datasets to identify trends and patterns
• Design and analyze A/B experiments
• Collaborate with product and engineering teams
• Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders
• Stay current with ML research and best practices
